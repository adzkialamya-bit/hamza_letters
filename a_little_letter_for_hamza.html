A little thank you

I'm glad our paths crossed.

Dear Hamza,

I don't really know where to begin, but I guess the simplest place is with two words: thank you.

Thank you for being someone I've had the chance to know. Thank you for the little moments, the conversations, and even the things that might seem ordinary but have somehow become meaningful to me.

Sometimes, we meet people without knowing how much of an impression they will leave on us. We don't always realize it in the moment, but certain people make our days a little brighter simply by being part of them.

And I think that's a beautiful thing.

I want you to know that I'm genuinely grateful our paths crossed. Whether we talk often or only occasionally, I hope you know that I wish nothing but good things for you.

A wish for you

May life be kind to you.

I hope life brings you happiness, gives you strength when things get difficult, and leads you toward all the dreams you've been quietly working for.

You deserve to experience the kind of happiness that makes you smile without having to explain why.

And if life ever gets overwhelming, I hope you remember that there are still people out there who sincerely wish you well, even from a distance.

Something from the heart

“Some feelings deserve to be expressed rather than left unspoken.”

Maybe this letter is a little sentimental. Maybe these words are more than I usually say out loud. But I think some feelings deserve to be expressed rather than left unspoken.

I don't want this message to be a goodbye, nor do I want it to be a promise about something neither of us can predict. I simply want it to be a little reminder that I'm glad to know you.

Maybe, someday

I'd love to know you a little more.

And honestly, I hope we get the chance to know each other better.

I'd love for there to be more conversations, more laughter, more little moments to look back on someday and smile about. There's something lovely about the possibility of two people becoming a little closer, one conversation at a time, without having to rush anything.

“No rush, no expectations. Just the possibility of something lovely.”

One last thing

Thank you for being part of my little world.

I hope this world gives you many reasons to be happy, and perhaps, if life is kind to us, it will give us more opportunities to share a few beautiful moments along the way.

Until then, take good care of yourself.

And if you ever wonder whether someone is wishing you happiness from somewhere in this world, I hope you'll remember this little letter.

With warmth, gratitude, and a quiet hope for the days ahead,

Someone who is glad to know you. ♡
